Our story

Inspired by place, crafted for gathering.

Golden River brings together the warmth of Kurdish hospitality, the elegance of modern cuisine, and the rhythm of shared tables. We craft every plate to feel generous, layered, and deeply personal.

From hand-prepared mezze to slow-roasted mains and delicate desserts, each dish is designed to reflect local ingredients, flavorful memory, and elevated hospitality.
